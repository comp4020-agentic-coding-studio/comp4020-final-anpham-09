## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, durability, and quiet safety of an intimate neighborhood circle. Rooted in a tactile storybook tradition, the aesthetic feels like a well-thumbed heirloom journal bound in bookcloth and lit by late-afternoon sun. It balances whimsy with grounded dignity—refusing hyper-polished tech sterility in favor of hand-touched warmth, organic restraint, and deep legibility.

### Design Movements
- **Tactile / Editorial Storybook:** Gentle, parchment-inspired surfaces, organic division lines, warm ambient drop shadows, and subtle paper-grain textures.
- **Humanist Craft:** High typographic clarity, sturdy proportions, and deliberate architectural references (open pantry shelving, quiet hearth tones, and grounded wooden anchors).

### Emotional Tone
- **Grounding & Safe:** Welcoming and comforting, conveying reliability across multiple generations (from grandparents to young parents).
- **Intimate & Calm:** Devoid of dopamine loops, garish alerts, or cold synthetic glass. Every interaction feels quiet, purposeful, and reassuring.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural domestic elements: fired clay, garden sage, sunlit amber resin, and iron-gall ink on unbleached linen.

- **Primary (`#C86446` - Terracotta Hearth):** The focal anchor. Used for primary narrative actions, core indicators, and welcoming emphasis. Reflects warmth, kiln-fired pottery, and human touch.
- **Secondary (`#5F7464` - Orchard Sage):** Evokes gardens, olive boughs, and quiet outdoor balance. Used for supportive actions, peaceful states, and verified community signals.
- **Tertiary (`#D9933B` - Pressed Amber):** An illuminating accent for moments of celebration, milestones, active highlights, and gentle warnings.
- **Neutral (`#2C2421` - Deep Ink Wood):** A deep, bark-infused charcoal ink rather than digital black. Preserves softness while guaranteeing AAA contrast against pale parchment backdrops.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#FBF8F2` - Soft Cream Linen):** An unbleached, comforting base tone reducing eye fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F4EFE6` - Pressed Oatmeal):** Used for cards, note slips, and inset panels.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#EDE4D6` - Sunlit Vellum):** Used for layered modals, active shelf compartments, and tactile hover treatments.
- **Subtle Hairline Border (`#E2D5C3` - Stitched Flax):** Low-contrast division lines keeping layouts organized without synthetic harshness.

## Typography

The typographic pairing contrasts literary warmth with crisp contemporary utility. 

- **Headlines (Merriweather):** Chosen for its sturdy serifs, generous x-height, and robust letterforms that mimic traditional letterpress. It creates rhythm, dignifies milestones, and lends a storybook cadence to community updates.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, humanist sans-serif with open apertures and warm terminals. It remains crisp on digital screens across all age groups while harmonizing with the editorial heft of the serif titles.
- **Editorial Details:** Chapter leads and memory dates may leverage italicized `Merriweather` (`headline-sm`) for thoughtful, journal-like pacing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a breathable 12-column system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column modular stack on mobile devices.

### Grid & Margins
- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** 12 columns, max content container width of 1200px, 2rem (`margin`) outer edge gutters, and 1.25rem (`gutter`) internal column spacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem outer canvas margin, 1rem column gutters.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem (`margin-mobile`) canvas margins, 0.875rem column gutters.

### Rhythm & Proportions
The layout leans into editorial pacing:
- Dense clusters of content (such as chore rosters or ingredient swaps) use tight `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps.
- Story entries, photo journals, and village announcements receive generous `space-xl` breathing room to encourage unhurried, calm browsing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use cold, synthetic shadows or floating glass blurs. Instead, depth relies on physical papercraft metaphors and soft parchment layering.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Hearth Floor):** Canvas base `#FBF8F2` with subtle woven grain.
- **Tier 1 (Parchment Card):** `#F4EFE6` bordered by a 1px solid hairline (`#E2D5C3`). Elevated with a soft, diffused ambient drop: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 36, 33, 0.05), 0px 4px 16px -4px rgba(44, 36, 33, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Raised Keepsake / Popover):** `#EDE4D6` with a firmer drop mimicking thick handmade paper: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 36, 33, 0.08), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(44, 36, 33, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modal / Village Ledger):** `#FBF8F2` centered above an organic ink overlay (`rgba(44, 36, 33, 0.45)` with soft 2px backdrop blur).

### Physical Motifs
- **Wood Shelf Dividers:** Horizontal boundaries often employ a warm dual-tone line (a 2px top stroke in `#E2D5C3` paired with an offset 1px highlight in `#FFFFFF`) suggesting hand-planed joinery.

## Shapes

With a roundedness factor of `2`, interface edges follow a gentle, tactile curve:
- **Default Containers & Buttons:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`), evoking smooth river pebbles and handcrafted wooden tokens.
- **Cards, Panels & Shelves:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), softening the composition without drifting into juvenile bubbly shapes.
- **Modals, Memory Keepsakes & Tags:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`), creating cozy, inviting dialogue surfaces.
- **Avatars & Pin Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`), framed with thin linen-colored borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Terracotta Hearth):** Solid `#C86446` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), `0.5rem` corner radius, internal padding of `0.75rem 1.5rem`. A discrete bottom inset border (`0px -2px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`) gives it subtle stamp-like tactility. Hover shifts to `#B65538`.
- **Secondary (Sage Branch):** Outlined with 1.5px solid `#5F7464`, tinted background `#5F746410`, text `#5F7464`.
- **Ghost (Linen Scribe):** No border, transparent background, text `#2C2421`, underlines on hover with a warm terracotta dot accent.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** `rounded-lg` with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding, using pastel washes over linen:
  - Active/Helper: Sage green background (`#5F746418`) with `#3E5343` text.
  - Urgent Need: Terracotta tint (`#C8644618`) with `#9A3E23` text.
  - Celebration/Milestone: Amber tint (`#D9933B22`) with `#8F5B19` text.

### Cards & Shelf Units
- Built from Tier 1 surfaces with `1rem` rounding.
- Headers within cards use `Merriweather` (`headline-md`) alongside a small leaf or hearth icon.
- Cards may feature a top "shelf lip" detail using a 3px accent stroke in muted wood tone (`#D8C4AC`).

### Lists & Rosters
- Separated not by generic gray lines, but by a 1px dashed or dotted flax rule (`#DDD0BE`).
- Alternating rows may feature an ultra-soft `#F7F3EB` wash for effortless tracking.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Crisp linen background (`#FFFFFF`), framed by 1.5px `#DCCEC0` borders with `0.5rem` rounding. Active focus changes border to `#C86446` with a soft 3px `#C8644620` outer ring.
- **Checkboxes:** Custom `0.375rem` rounded squares with an earthy clay fill (`#C86446`) and a hand-drawn-style checkmark icon.

### Dedicated Village Components
- **The Pantry Ledger:** A structured table for mutual aid (lending tools, meal trains), featuring soft sage badges and card dividers.
- **Memory Slips:** Informal, sticky-note inspired containers rotated subtly (`-0.5deg` to `0.5deg`) with a pale parchment background (`#FFFDF8`) and soft ambient shadows.
