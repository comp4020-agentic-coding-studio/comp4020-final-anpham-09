The Living Courtyard & Ancestral Room

The heart of Nghe An House has neither locks nor heavy doors. Rafters of dark weathered cedar carry seventy years of woodsmoke. Here, family members wander in without knocking, check the ceramic water jar, and sit quietly beside whoever is peeling fruit on the veranda.
